# Supply Chain Q&A — Master Question Bank

This is the master list of questions the probing agent will ask. Questions are grouped by domain. The agent should adapt its flow based on the user's role and previous answers.

---

## Category 1: General Overview

1. What does your company manufacture or sell?
2. How many locations (warehouses, factories, offices) does your company operate from?
3. How many suppliers does your company work with regularly?
4. What is your approximate annual revenue range?
5. How many people are involved in your supply chain operations day-to-day?

## Category 2: Procurement & Sourcing

6. How do you currently find and choose new suppliers?
7. Do you have a formal process for evaluating supplier performance? If yes, how often?
8. What are the top 3 challenges you face when sourcing raw materials or products?
9. How far in advance do you typically place orders with suppliers?
10. Do you rely on a single supplier for any critical material? If yes, which ones?
11. How do you handle price negotiations with suppliers today?
12. Have you experienced any major supplier failures or disruptions in the last 2 years?

## Category 3: Inventory Management

13. How do you currently track your inventory? (e.g., spreadsheets, software, paper)
14. How often do you run into stockout situations (running out of critical items)?
15. How often do you deal with excess or obsolete inventory?
16. What is your average inventory holding period (in days or weeks)?
17. Do you use any forecasting method to predict demand? If yes, what kind?
18. How do you decide how much safety stock to keep?

## Category 4: Warehousing & Storage

19. How many warehouses or storage facilities do you operate?
20. What is the approximate total storage capacity you use?
21. What are the biggest pain points in your warehouse operations today?
22. Do you use any warehouse management system (WMS)? If yes, which one?
23. How do you handle damaged or returned goods in your warehouse?

## Category 5: Logistics & Transportation

24. How do you ship your products to customers? (own fleet, third-party, mix)
25. What percentage of deliveries arrive on time currently?
26. What is your average delivery lead time from order to customer?
27. What are your top 3 logistics challenges right now?
28. Do you ship internationally? If yes, what are the main difficulties?
29. How do you currently track shipments once they leave your facility?

## Category 6: Technology & Systems

30. What software tools do you use for supply chain management today?
31. Are your systems integrated with each other, or do you manually transfer data between them?
32. What manual or repetitive tasks take up the most time for your team?
33. Have you tried automating any part of your supply chain? If yes, what?
34. What is the single biggest technology gap in your supply chain operations?

## Category 7: Risk & Compliance

35. What are the top 3 risks you worry about in your supply chain?
36. Do you have a business continuity plan for supply chain disruptions?
37. Are there any regulatory or compliance requirements you must follow? Which ones?
38. How do you currently monitor for potential supply chain risks?

## Category 8: Goals & Priorities

39. If you could fix one thing about your supply chain tomorrow, what would it be?
40. What are your supply chain goals for the next 12 months?
41. Are you currently evaluating any new tools or technologies? If yes, which ones?
42. What does "success" look like for your supply chain team this year?
43. Is there anything else about your supply chain that you think is important for us to know?
