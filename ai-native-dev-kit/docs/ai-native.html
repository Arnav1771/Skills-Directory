Methodology & Assumptions

How the model works

Scope is held constant across both delivery models and expressed in ideal person-days. A team of N FTEs produces N person-days of progress per calendar working day (productive capacity assumed at 100%). Calendar duration is found by a day-by-day simulation that accumulates progress until the rework-inflated scope is met.

  • Traditional: duration = scope·(1+rework_T) / N_T; cost = N_T · duration · rate_T.
  • AI-native: throughput is multiplied by an uplift U that ramps linearly from 1.0× to full over the adoption period; cost adds a daily platform/token run-rate.
  • ROI is net cash saving divided by AI-specific incremental spend (platform run-rate).
  • Breakeven uplift is solved by bisection: the full uplift at which AI-native total cost equals the traditional baseline.
Why the gain isn't only "uplift"

The waterfall separates three levers that are often conflated: team compression (fewer, more senior people), throughput uplift (more output per engineer), and quality (lower rework). When your AI-native pod is already smaller than the baseline team, a large share of savings comes from compression — independent of any per-engineer speed-up. Surfacing this prevents over-crediting the AI tooling.

Calibrating the uplift assumption

Per-engineer productivity gains are real but highly task-dependent and non-uniform. Greenfield, boilerplate-heavy, well-specified work sees the largest gains; complex, legacy, or ambiguous work sees far less — and controlled studies have shown experienced engineers occasionally slower on intricate tasks. Early gains in the 30–50% range commonly compress as the easy wins are exhausted. Treat 1.3× as a defensible floor for mixed enterprise work and reserve 3×+ for narrow, favourable conditions.

Use as a planning lens, not a guarantee. The uplift multiplier is the single most influential and most uncertain input — always read the headline alongside the sensitivity chart and the breakeven uplift, and pressure-test whether your scope mix justifies the chosen multiplier.
